Privacy Policy

How Entrepreneur Wealth Creation collects, uses, and protects the information you provide through our website and membership application.

Contents

Information We Collect How We Use It EmailJS & Transmission Data Sharing Data Retention Your Rights Security Cookies Contact Us

Last updated: 18 February 2026  ·  Entrepreneur Wealth Creation (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa

1 Information We Collect

When you submit a membership application through our website, we collect the following information:

  • Registered company name and primary business sector
  • Years in operation and annual revenue range
  • Availability of audited financial statements
  • CEO or founder's full name and title
  • Business email address and company website URL
  • Company overview and description of operations
  • Indicative revenue figures for the prior three financial years
  • EBITDA range and capital being sought
  • Strategic intent and key growth milestones

We do not collect identity documents, banking details, passwords, or any sensitive personal information at the application stage.

2 How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used exclusively to:

  • Assess your eligibility for EWC membership
  • Contact you to discuss your application or arrange a Discovery Call
  • Maintain an internal record of your application for our review team
🔒

Your information is never used for marketing purposes, sold to third parties, or shared outside of the EWC review team without your explicit consent.

3 EmailJS & Data Transmission

Our membership application form uses EmailJS — a third-party email delivery service — to transmit your submitted information securely to our internal review team at info@entrepreneurwealthcreation.co.za.

When you submit the form, your data is sent via EmailJS's servers and delivered to our inbox. EmailJS processes data in accordance with their own privacy policy, which can be reviewed at emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not store your application data on our own servers. The information resides in our email account and any internal records maintained by our review team.

4 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your application data is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within EWC: Members of our internal review team who assess applications on a need-to-know basis
  • EmailJS: As the technical service used to deliver your application to us (see Section 3)
  • Legal obligation: If required by applicable South African law or a valid court order

If your application progresses to the due diligence stage, any further information shared at that point will be governed by a formal Non-Disclosure Agreement.

5 Data Retention

Application data is retained for a period of 12 months from the date of submission. After this period, data is securely deleted from our systems unless an active membership or business relationship has been established, in which case data is retained for the duration of that relationship and for a further period as required by applicable law.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see Section 9).

6 Your Rights

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 9.

7 Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website is served over HTTPS and application data is transmitted via encrypted channels through EmailJS.

However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics that collect personal information without consent.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google's CDN, which may set a cookie in accordance with Google's privacy policy. No other third-party cookies are used on this site.
