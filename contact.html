Membership Eligibility Criteria

Revenue Threshold

Minimum annual business revenue of R5 million. Demonstrated capacity, not early-stage projection.

Minimum: R5M+ per annum

Business Tenure

Minimum 2–3 years of continuous, verifiable business operation with documented financial history.

Minimum: 2–3 Years Operation

Professional Infrastructure

A functioning commercial operation, not home-based or freelance. Professional systems and staffing required.

Commercial Operation Required

Applications that do not meet the above criteria will not progress to advisory review. EWC maintains strict admission standards to protect the integrity and institutional credibility of the membership network. All information provided is treated with complete confidentiality.
